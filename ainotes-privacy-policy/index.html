Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

Welcome to AiNotes. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy describes how Elecist ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use the AiNotes mobile, desktop, and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign up for an account, we collect your email address, password, and display name. If you use Google Sign-In, we collect information provided by Google, including your email and public profile information.

Notes and Content

We collect and store the text, formatting, and assets (images/files) you create or upload within the application to provide you with the core functionality and cross-device synchronization.

Usage Information

We collect anonymous analytical data about how you interact with our application to improve user experience, including screen views and feature usage.

2. How We Store Your Data

To ensure your notes are accessible across all your devices, your data is securely stored in the following cloud environments:

  • Google Firebase: Handles authentication (Auth) and real-time database (Firestore) for your notes text and metadata.
  • AWS S3: Securely stores file attachments and media uploaded to your notes.

3. Permissions and Privacy

AiNotes may request the following permissions on your device:

  • Internet: Required for cross-device synchronization.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver reminders you have explicitly scheduled.
  • Storage/Photos: Needed to allow you to upload attachments and images to your notes.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data is strictly used for providing the synchronization and features of the app.

5. Security

Calculated security measures are used to protect your information, including encryption for data in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your account and all associated data at any time through the application settings or by contacting us.
